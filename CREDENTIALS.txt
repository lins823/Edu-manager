WHAT EACH ROLE CAN DO
Admin ........ Create users, terms, classes; view hostels; change school
               name, motto and logo; reset any password.
Principal .... Search students, fee and exam summaries, staff list.
               Only the Principal can reactivate an inactive student.
Deputy ....... Record discipline cases. 3 cases in a term = automatic
               suspension. Can view all students (read-only).
Teacher ...... Own class list, attendance, exams and marks, class fee
               statement, add/remove student requests to the Bursar.
Boarding ..... Hostel occupancy, boarders list, move students between
               hostels. (Boys: Lions, Tigers, Eagles, Hawks - 40 each.
               Girls: Roses, Lilies - 70 each.)
Bursar ....... Register students (1 or 2 parents, hostel assigned by
               gender), update fees, approve or reject teacher requests.
Parent ....... View only their own children: bio, results, fees,
               discipline, attendance.
